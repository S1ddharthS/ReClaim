BUG-01 Done (fix verified) Jira Key: RECL-33 • Priority: High • Points: 2

Reserved Stock Incorrectly Remains Available in Inventory

Scenario: A material listing has 100 units available. A buyer requests 70 units. The supplier approves the reservation. The system previously updated the reservation record but forgot to atomically update the surplus listing inventory, leaving 100 units still marked as available.

❌ Faulty Behaviour (Before Fix)

Available: 100 (Unchanged)
Reserved: 70
Danger: Duplicate reservations could oversell stock beyond physical inventory!

✅ Verified Fix (Atomic Decrement)

Available: 30 (100 - 70)
Reserved: 70
Guards: Negative quantity blocked, idempotency check prevents double holds.
